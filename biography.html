Pharm. Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh

Biography

Pharm. Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh

Pharm. Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh is a pharmacist, businessman, traditional title holder and public figure from Idah, Kogi State.

His professional background includes pharmacy, pharmaceutical business, distribution and community activities.

Date of Birth 25 March 1974
Place of Birth Idah, Kogi State
Profession Pharmacist / Businessman
University University of Nigeria, Nsukka

Early Life and Background

Pharm. Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh was born on 25 March 1974 in Idah, Kogi State, into the family of the late Chief Charles O. Iduh and Mrs. Anasthesia Iduh.

He began his education at Federal Polytechnic Staff School, Idah, and subsequently attended Federal Government College, Ugwolawo, where he belonged to the 1992 set.

The published biography describes his early years as characterised by academic performance, hard work and participation in student affairs.

Education and Student Leadership

In 1995, Peter Iduh proceeded to the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN) to study Pharmacy.

During his university years, he became involved in student leadership and represented his faculty in the university's Student Senate.

He eventually served as Deputy Senate President.

According to the published biography, he advocated for pharmacy students and used motions and lobbying in addressing accommodation challenges affecting students.

He graduated in 2002 with a Bachelor of Pharmacy degree.

Professional Training:
After graduation, he completed his mandatory pharmacy internship at the Federal Medical Centre, Asaba, Delta State, followed by his National Youth Service Corps programme in Kaduna.

Pharmaceutical and Business Career

After completing his NYSC programme, Pharm. Iduh entered the pharmaceutical industry as a sales representative.

The published biography states that his performance led to his promotion to Assistant Manager in less than two years.

He later moved into private business and became Managing Director of Swan Medico Ltd, a pharmaceutical company described as being involved in importing and distributing fertility-related pharmaceutical products.

The biography identifies Ferring Pharmaceutical of Switzerland and Vitrolife of Sweden among companies whose products were distributed through the business in Nigeria.

He is also identified as Chairman/CEO of Iduh Integrated Services Nigeria Ltd, Frepet Nig. Ltd and Iduh Pharmacy Ltd.

Iduh Integrated Services has been described as a distributor in the FCT and Kogi State for products associated with companies including Unilever, GSK Healthcare, Perfetti Van Melle, Kimberly-Clark and Suntory.

According to the published biography, he also holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from Business School Netherlands.

Traditional Title and Community Activities

Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh is associated with the traditional title Ogah Attah of the Igala Kingdom (Edeee).

The published profile also identifies him with the title Ese Rovia 1 of Agbarha-Otor Kingdom.

Public reports have described him as a businessman, pharmacist and philanthropist involved in community and grassroots activities in Kogi East.

His community activities and public engagements have included participation in events and initiatives across Kogi East.

Political Activities

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh has also participated in partisan political activities.

In May 2026, Kogi Reports reported that he won the African Democratic Congress (ADC) primary for the Idah Federal Constituency House of Representatives seat.

The reported constituency covers Idah, Ibaji, Igalamela-Odolu and Ofu Local Government Areas.

Recent September 2026 reporting has also identified him publicly as the ADC candidate for the Idah Federal Constituency at the 2026 Igala Nation's Cup event in Idah.

Current public profile:
As reported in 2026, Pharm. Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh is associated with the ADC candidacy for the Idah Federal Constituency House of Representatives seat.

Family

The published biography states that Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh is married to Mrs. Ruth Ileigo Iduh (née Obaje).

The same published biography states that they have children named Ojochelemi, Enyonedugbojo, Omakojiata and Achile.

Biography Summary

Pharm. Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh is a pharmacist, entrepreneur, traditional title holder and philanthropist from Idah, Kogi State.

His career has included pharmaceutical practice, business management, distribution and community activities.

He has also participated in political and grassroots affairs in Kogi East and, according to 2026 public reports, is associated with the African Democratic Congress candidacy for Idah Federal Constituency.
