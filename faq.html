Frequently Asked Questions

This page provides general information for visitors. Additional verified information can be published as it becomes available.

Who is Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh?

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh is presented on this website as a House of Representatives candidate for Idah Federal Constituency under the African Democratic Congress (ADC).

Which constituency is represented on this website?

The website concerns Idah Federal Constituency and the communities identified here as Idah, Ibaji, Igalamela-Odolu and Ofu.

What issues are discussed on the website?

The website discusses areas of public interest including healthcare, education, roads and infrastructure, agriculture, employment, youth development, women's participation and support for local businesses.

What does responsible representation mean on this website?

The website uses the term to refer to activities such as listening to constituents, participating in legislation, advocating on issues affecting communities, maintaining communication and promoting accountability.

Where can I find photographs?

Photographs and other visual materials can be viewed on the Gallery page of this website.

Where can I find news and press information?

The News and Press pages are available through the website menu. Verified public updates can be published there when available.

How can I make contact?

Visitors can use the Contact page or call the listed contact number: +234 803 390 6752.

Does the website collect voting information?

This website does not provide a voter-registration or voter-preference collection service. Visitors should use the appropriate official electoral channels for election-related processes and voter services.

Will more information be added?

Yes. Public information, verified statements, photographs, documents and other website materials may be added as they become available.
