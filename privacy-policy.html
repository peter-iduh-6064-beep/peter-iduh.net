Privacy Policy

Information about how personal information may be collected, used, stored and protected through this website.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how this website may handle information provided by visitors of the official website of Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh.

The website provides public information concerning the candidate, constituency-related issues, photographs, news, media materials, public statements and contact information.

2. Information We May Collect

When website forms are connected to the website's database services, visitors may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, where requested
  • Subject of an enquiry
  • Message or enquiry
  • Other information voluntarily submitted by the visitor

Visitors should only provide information that is necessary for the purpose of their enquiry.

3. Contact and Enquiry Forms

The website may provide forms through which visitors can send enquiries, messages or other permitted information.

When these forms are connected to a database service such as Supabase, information submitted through the forms may be securely transmitted to and stored in the configured database.

The exact information collected will depend on the fields included in each form.

4. Use of Information

Information voluntarily submitted through website forms may be used for legitimate website-related purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries
  • Communicating with visitors
  • Managing website enquiries
  • Providing requested information
  • Improving website services

5. Database and Supabase

The website may use Supabase as a backend database and application service for storing information submitted through connected forms.

Supabase may process information according to its own privacy, security and data-processing policies. Where Supabase is used, the website administrator will configure access permissions and database security controls appropriate to the website's needs.

The website should not collect unnecessary sensitive personal information through its forms.

6. Data Access

Information submitted through website forms may be accessible to authorized website administrators or persons responsible for managing enquiries and website operations.

Access to database information should be restricted through appropriate authentication and database permissions.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

When Supabase is connected, database security rules, authentication, access policies and other available security controls should be configured appropriately before the website is placed into full production use.

8. Data Retention

Information submitted through website forms may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to handle enquiries, maintain website records or meet legitimate administrative requirements.

Retention periods may be reviewed as the website's services and database structure develop.

9. Request for Removal

Where applicable, a visitor may contact the website administrator to request information concerning personal data submitted through the website.

Requests may be reviewed before information is removed, particularly where legal, security or administrative requirements apply.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use basic browser technologies required for normal website operation. If analytics, advertising, authentication or other third-party services are introduced in the future, this policy may be updated to explain their use.

11. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services for hosting, databases, images, communication or other website functions.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and terms of service. Visitors should review the relevant policies when using those external services.

12. External Links

This website may contain links to external websites or social-media platforms. The website administrator is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

13. Children's Privacy

The website is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Visitors should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information.

14. Political and Election Information

This website may publish public information concerning political activities, public statements, constituency issues and election- related information.

The website should not request unnecessary sensitive personal information from visitors. In particular, visitors should not submit passwords, financial credentials, official identification numbers or other highly sensitive information through ordinary contact forms.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, database services, forms or information-handling practices change.

When significant changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page.

16. Contact

For questions concerning this Privacy Policy or information submitted through the website, visitors may use the contact information provided below.

Important Notice

This Privacy Policy is prepared for the planned website structure. When Supabase, authentication, forms, analytics or other third-party services are actually connected, the policy should be reviewed against the final technical setup before the website goes live.
