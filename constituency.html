Idah Federal Constituency

Idah Federal Constituency brings together communities across Idah, Ibaji, Igalamela-Odolu and Ofu. The constituency is home to diverse communities, families, businesses, farmers, professionals, young people and women whose interests form an important part of the conversation about representation.

The Four Areas

Different communities, shared interests and common development concerns.

IDAH

Community & Development

Idah is one of the communities within the federal constituency whose residents have interests in education, healthcare, infrastructure, employment and economic opportunities.

Effective representation involves listening to community concerns and communicating them through appropriate legislative channels.

IBAJI

Community & Development

Ibaji includes many rural communities where agriculture, roads, healthcare, education and access to basic infrastructure are important areas of public discussion.

Community needs can be communicated through representation and appropriate advocacy for development opportunities.

IGALAMELA-ODOLU

Community & Development

Communities across Igalamela-Odolu contribute to the wider social and economic life of the constituency.

Infrastructure, agriculture, education, healthcare and opportunities for local businesses remain important areas for public conversation.

OFU

Community & Development

Ofu communities are part of the wider constituency and have interests in infrastructure, education, agriculture, healthcare and economic opportunities.

Listening to communities provides a basis for understanding their priorities and concerns.

One Constituency. A Shared Future.

Idah, Ibaji, Igalamela-Odolu and Ofu are different communities within one federal constituency. An informed conversation about representation can consider the interests, needs and aspirations of people across all four areas.
