About Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh

Learn more about the candidate and his public profile.

Public Profile

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh is a House of Representatives candidate for Idah Federal Constituency under the African Democratic Congress (ADC).

The Idah Federal Constituency comprises Idah, Ibaji, Igalamela-Odolu and Ofu Local Government Areas.

This website provides a central platform for information about his public activities, constituency priorities, vision, media updates and election-related information.
