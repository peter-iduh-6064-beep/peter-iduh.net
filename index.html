About Pharm. Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh

Professional, community and public profile

Pharmacist • Entrepreneur • Community Figure

Pharm. Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh is a pharmacist and businessman associated with pharmaceutical and distribution activities.

Public profiles also describe his involvement in community and grassroots activities in Kogi East.

He is associated with the Idah Federal Constituency, comprising Idah, Ibaji, Igalamela-Odolu and Ofu Local Government Areas.

His professional background includes pharmacy, pharmaceutical distribution, business administration and community engagement.
