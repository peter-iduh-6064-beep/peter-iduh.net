About Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh is a House of Representatives candidate for Idah Federal Constituency under the African Democratic Congress (ADC).

The constituency comprises Idah, Ibaji, Igalamela-Odolu and Ofu Local Government Areas.

This website provides information about the candidate, constituency, public activities, announcements, media materials and other relevant information.
