A Vision for Responsible Representation

The race for the House of Representatives seat in Idah Federal Constituency brings renewed attention to the kind of leadership and representation residents want to see in the National Assembly.

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh, contesting under the African Democratic Congress (ADC), is presenting himself to voters across Idah, Ibaji, Igalamela-Odolu and Ofu as one of the candidates seeking their mandate.

The conversation should include representation, accountability, development and issues affecting everyday life across the constituency.

Vision & Representation

Information on the principles of responsible representation.

Responsible Representation

The responsibility of a federal representative includes giving voice to constituents, participating in legislation, advocating for appropriate federal projects and maintaining communication with the communities they represent.


Constituents can examine candidates' experience, proposals, priorities and commitments as part of an informed public conversation about representation.
