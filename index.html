About Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh is a House of Representatives candidate for Idah Federal Constituency.

The constituency comprises Idah, Ibaji, Igalamela-Odolu and Ofu Local Government Areas.

This website provides information about the candidate, constituency, public priorities, activities, news, events and election-result information.
