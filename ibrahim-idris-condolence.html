BREAKING: PHARM. PETER YAKUBU IDUH MOURNS THE PASSING OF FORMER KOGI STATE GOVERNOR, ALHAJI IBRAHIM IDRIS

After learning of the passing through a report carried by Ibaji TV Channel, Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh, ADC House of Representatives Candidate for Idah Federal Constituency, extends his deepest condolences to the Idris family and the people of Kogi State.
Published: September 2026

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh has expressed his heartfelt condolences to the family of former Executive Governor of Kogi State, Alhaji Ibrahim Idris, following the report of his passing carried by Ibaji TV Channel.

In his condolence message, Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh described the passing as a moment of sorrow for the family, friends, associates and the people of Kogi State who knew and interacted with the former governor.

He extended his sympathy to the immediate family of Alhaji Ibrahim Idris, praying that Almighty God grants the family the strength and comfort to bear the loss.

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh also prayed for the peaceful repose of the deceased and encouraged the family and well-wishers to remain strong during this difficult period.

The report of the passing was carried by Ibaji TV Channel.

May Almighty God grant Alhaji Ibrahim Idris eternal rest and grant his family, friends and loved ones the fortitude to bear the loss.

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh
ADC Candidate for House of Representatives
Idah Federal Constituency
