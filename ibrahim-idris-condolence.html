CONDOLENCE MESSAGE

BREAKING: PHARM. PETER YAKUBU IDUH MOURNS THE PASSING OF FORMER KOGI STATE GOVERNOR, ALHAJI IBRAHIM IDRIS

After Learning of the Passing Through a Report Carried by Ibaji TV Channel, Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh, ADC House of Representatives Candidate for Idah Federal Constituency, Extends His Deepest Condolences to the Idris Family and the People of Kogi State
Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh

Having learnt of the passing of His Excellency, Alhaji Ibrahim Idris, former Executive Governor of Kogi State , through a report carried by Ibaji TV Channel, I, Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh, wish to express my deepest condolences to the immediate family, relatives, friends, associates, and the entire people of Kogi State over this painful loss.

The passing of a former governor and prominent public figure is a moment of deep sadness and reflection. At such a difficult time, words can hardly express the depth of grief felt by the family and all those who knew and worked with him.

His Excellency, Alhaji Ibrahim Idris, occupies an important place in the political history of Kogi State. As a former Executive Governor, he served the people of the state at an important period and contributed to the administration and development of Kogi State.

Beyond his public position, however, he was first and foremost a member of a family. His passing represents an immeasurable personal loss to his children, grandchildren, relatives, friends, and loved ones who shared his life and memories.

As a fellow son of Kogi State, I extend my sincere sympathy to the entire Idris family. I pray that Almighty God will grant the family the strength, courage, and fortitude to bear this painful loss.

May God comfort his children and loved ones, grant them peace during this difficult period, and preserve the cherished memories of his life and service.

The people of Kogi State will continue to remember his period of service as part of the political history of our state. As we mourn his departure, we should also reflect on the responsibilities of leadership and the importance of service to humanity.

His Excellency, Alhaji Ibrahim Idris, former Executive Governor of Kogi State.

On behalf of myself, my family, associates, and supporters, I extend my heartfelt condolences to the Idris family, the people of Kogi State, and everyone affected by this loss.

May Almighty God forgive his shortcomings, accept his good deeds, grant him eternal rest, and give his family the strength to bear this irreparable loss.

May his soul rest in perfect peace.

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh
ADC Candidate for House of Representatives
Idah Federal Constituency
The report was carried by Ibaji TV Channel.
