About Pharm. Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh

House of Representatives Candidate

Pharm. Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh

Pharm. Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh is a pharmacist, entrepreneur and businessman from Idah, Kogi State.

He is publicly associated with community, professional, business and political activities in Kogi East.

He is also publicly reported as the African Democratic Congress (ADC) candidate for the Idah Federal Constituency House of Representatives seat in 2026.

Background

Born on 25 March 1974 in Idah, Kogi State, Peter Yakubu Iduh began his education at Federal Polytechnic Staff School, Idah, before attending Federal Government College, Ugwolawo.

He subsequently studied Pharmacy at the University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN), graduating with a Bachelor of Pharmacy degree in 2002.

His professional career has included pharmaceutical practice, sales, management, distribution and private business.

Professional Profile

Pharm. Iduh has been associated with pharmaceutical and distribution businesses, including Swan Medico Ltd and Iduh Integrated Services Nigeria Ltd.

His published biography also identifies him with Frepet Nig. Ltd and Iduh Pharmacy Ltd and records an MBA from Business School Netherlands.

Community and Public Activities

Chief Peter Yakubu Iduh is associated with the traditional title Ogah Attah of the Igala Kingdom (Edeee).

Public profiles have described him as a businessman, pharmacist and philanthropist involved in community and grassroots activities.

Political Profile

In 2026, public reports stated that Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh won the African Democratic Congress primary for the Idah Federal Constituency House of Representatives seat.

The reported constituency comprises:

IDAH
IBAJI
IGALAMELA-ODOLU
OFU
