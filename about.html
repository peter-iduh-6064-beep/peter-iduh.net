About the Candidate

Information presented on this website about Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh and his public campaign.

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh

Pharm. Peter Yakubu Iduh is contesting for the House of Representatives seat representing Idah Federal Constituency under the African Democratic Congress (ADC).

The constituency includes Idah, Ibaji, Igalamela-Odolu and Ofu.

His public campaign provides an opportunity for constituents to examine issues affecting their communities and consider different proposals for representation.

Political Platform:
African Democratic Congress (ADC)

Responsible Representation

The responsibilities of a federal representative include several areas of public service.

Listening

Maintaining communication with communities and understanding issues raised by constituents.

Legislation

Participating in legislative activities and contributing to discussions on national issues.

Advocacy

Raising constituency concerns and advocating for appropriate development initiatives.

Communication

Keeping communities informed about relevant public and legislative activities.

Accountability

Promoting transparency and maintaining an open conversation with constituents.

Public Interest

Keeping public needs and constituency concerns part of the representation conversation.
